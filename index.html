About

WHO I AM.
WHAT I DO.

I’m Veda Bharghav, a data engineer and analyst who works across data engineering, business intelligence, operational analytics, automation, and applied AI.

I build the layers between raw information and a useful decision: ingestion, transformation, validation, data modeling, governed reporting, analytics products, and the interfaces people actually use. My experience spans enterprise reporting, renewable-energy manufacturing, healthcare and partner-data integration, and technical support environments.

My core stack includes SQL, Python, Microsoft Fabric, Snowflake, Power BI, Tableau, ETL/ELT, dimensional modeling, and analytics automation. Outside of data, photography is the other half of how I work — noticing detail, composition, timing, and the story inside a frame.
